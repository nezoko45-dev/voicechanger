How it works

  1. Browser microphone stays open.
  2. Silence detection captures each sentence as WAV.
  3. Cartesia STT turns speech into text.
  4. Cartesia TTS speaks it with your selected voice.
  5. The WAV plays through your selected Windows output.

No MelonLoader, DLL, C#, Python, or desktop bridge is used by this app.
