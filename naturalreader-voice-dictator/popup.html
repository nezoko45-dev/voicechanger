NaturalReader Voice Dictator

Open the NaturalReader Online Reader. Open its “Type or Paste Text” editor, then use the floating Voice Dictator panel.

  1. Click Start dictation and allow microphone access.
  2. Speak; the recognized words are inserted into the text field.
  3. After you pause, the extension tries to click Play automatically.

Tip: If automatic Play can't identify the button, the text stays in the field so you can click Play yourself.
