How this works
  1. Open TTS Voice Wizard and enable its WebSocket-to-TTS listener.
  2. Keep its WebSocket port at 9008 unless you changed it.
  3. Click CONNECT, then START.
  4. Speak. Moonshine sends finalized lines to Voice Wizard for TTS.
