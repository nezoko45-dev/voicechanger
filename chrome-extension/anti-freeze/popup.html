Anti-Freeze Tabs

Keep important tabs from being discarded

Paste the full https:// or http:// URL of the tool you need while playing VRChat.

Your tabs

    For Zolt TTS: In Chrome, open Settings → Performance → “Always keep these sites active” and add Zolt TTS's website. This Chrome setting is important because this extension cannot turn off background JavaScript throttling.
    This extension asks Chrome not to automatically discard protected tabs. It cannot guarantee that background pages keep running at full speed or prevent every kind of freeze.
