🎙 Pocket TTS Instant Voice Changer

Raw Chrome microphone → silence detection → speech text → Pocket TTS cloned voice → converted audio player. Your raw microphone is never played back.

Each time you speak, the cloned Pocket TTS result is placed here and played. This player contains the converted voice, not your raw microphone.
Press START, then choose your reference WAV. Speak into the selected microphone; after a short silence, Pocket TTS generates your cloned voice.
The Pocket TTS browser implementation uses an INT8 model and streams generated chunks from a Web Worker. The clone/reference voice stays loaded for repeated utterances.
