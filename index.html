🎀 WAV Voice Changer

No Python. No terminal. No Deepgram Flux. Use your WAV as a voice reference, transcribe it in your browser, then create new speech with a browser-based voice-cloning tool.
🌐 Browser-based • No Python server • WAV supported
  1. 1Transcribe your WAV
    Upload your WAV to a browser STT tool and copy the transcript.
  2. 2Clone the voice
    Upload the same WAV as the voice sample to a browser voice-cloning tool.
  3. 3Enter the transcript
    Paste the transcript or type completely new text, then generate speech.
  4. 4Download WAV
    Play or download the synthesized WAV from the cloning tool.
