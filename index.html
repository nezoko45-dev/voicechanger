Live pipeline
Waiting for microphone…

The microphone stays open continuously. Deepgram transcribes the live stream, Deepgram TTS synthesizes the finalized speech, and OpenVoice V2 converts that TTS audio into the selected reference voice. Audio is buffered and processed through a continuous queue.

FP32 model build

OpenVoice V2 stays FP32 and runs through ONNX Runtime WASM. Deepgram handles the streaming speech recognition and natural TTS stage; OpenVoice supplies the voice cloning stage.

Important for VRChat

Select the Voicemeeter output that feeds the microphone device used by VRChat. The converted signal is ordinary audio routed through Windows/Voicemeeter.
