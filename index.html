🎙 Pocket TTS Instant Voice Changer

Raw Chrome microphone → silence detection → speech text → Pocket TTS using your WAV clone → converted voice. Your raw microphone is never played back.

The player contains the generated Pocket TTS voice. TEST WAV VOICE verifies that your reference WAV is actually being used before you speak.
Press START, choose your reference WAV, then speak. Pocket TTS will generate the cloned voice after the selected silence.
Pocket TTS stays loaded and the WAV clone stays cached for repeated utterances.
