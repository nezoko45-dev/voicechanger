ElevenLabs Voice Dictation

Open and sign in to the ElevenLabs Text to Speech page, then open this extension's dictation side panel. The side panel keeps speech recognition separate from the generator page so you can switch to VRChat.

Open the side panel, click Start dictation there once and allow microphone access. Keep the side panel open while playing VRChat. The floating button on ElevenLabs can toggle dictation, but speech recognition runs in the side panel so it is less likely to stop when you switch away from ElevenLabs.
