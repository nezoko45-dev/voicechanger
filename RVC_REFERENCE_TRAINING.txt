RVC REFERENCE TRAINING

1. Run setup_rvc.bat once.
2. Run train_reference_rvc.bat.
3. The script converts Recording (10).wav to rvc-dataset\reference.wav.
4. The RVC WebUI opens.

Training values:
- Model/experiment name: uwu_girl
- Dataset folder: <voicechanger repo>\rvc-dataset
- Sample rate: 40k
- Version: v2
- F0/pitch method: RMVPE

RVC recommends at least about 10 minutes of clean, low-noise speech for a small-data training set.
A short/noisy reference can still run, but the resulting voice model may be weaker.

After training:
- The inference-ready .pth belongs in rvc-realtime\assets\weights
- The generated index belongs in rvc-realtime\assets\indices
- Use rvc_realtime.bat for live conversion.

IMPORTANT:
Recording (10).wav in this repo is mislabeled: its container is MP4 even though its filename ends in .wav.
The training launcher converts it to a genuine mono PCM WAV before RVC uses it.
