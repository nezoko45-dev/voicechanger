tts.dev Voice Dictation

Speak naturally; the extension types your words and clicks Stream after you pause.

Hands-free dictation

Keep tts.dev open in a Chrome tab. Start listening here, then speak. After about 3 seconds of silence, the extension submits the recognized sentence and clicks “Stream”.

Ready. Open tts.dev, then start listening.

How to use

1
Open tts.dev

Sign in if needed and leave the page open with its text box and Stream button visible.

2
Allow microphone access

Click Start listening and allow Chrome to use your microphone when prompted.

3
Speak and pause

Your transcript appears in the text box. A pause of about 3 seconds triggers Stream automatically. Keep the extension and tts.dev open.

Note: tts.dev’s live page structure could not be verified here. If its text field or Stream control differs from the expected labels, the status will report that it could not find the control.
